Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question here? Send me a message and I'll get back to you within one business day.

What's the difference between Launch and the System?

Launch gets you a website that collects quote requests, with an instant price calculator, photo quotes and a Lead Inbox. The Booked Jobs System adds what happens next: online booking, instant replies, Google review requests and reminders that bring customers back every year.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and once the final payment is made, the website and all of its files belong to you. No lock-in, no strings attached.

Do I need the monthly Care plan?

No, your site works without it. Care covers hosting, updates and the things that run in the background, like text alerts, review requests and repeat-job reminders. It's $79 a month, billed month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

Will the quote calculator show the right prices?

Yes. We set it up with your real starting prices and size ranges, and it always shows a range, not a fixed price. You confirm the final quote, so you're never locked into a number.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just your business name or Google Maps link. I write the copy from your services and reviews. If you have before and after photos or a logo, send them over and I'll put them to good use.

How does payment work?

You pay by card or bank transfer through a secure invoice: 50% after you approve your preview and the remaining 50% on launch day. No payment is required for the preview.

What if I don't like the preview?

Then you don't owe me anything. The preview is completely free and comes with no obligation. If you move forward, two rounds of revisions make sure every detail is right.
