Frequently asked questions

Don't see your question here? Send me a message and I'll get back to you within one business day.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and once the final payment is made, the website and all of its files belong to you. No lock-in, no strings attached.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just your business name or Google Maps link. I write the copy based on your services and customer reviews. If you have photos of your work or a logo, send them over and I'll put them to good use.

How does payment work?

You pay by card or bank transfer through a secure invoice: 50% after you approve your preview and the remaining 50% on launch day. No payment is required for the preview.

What if I don't like the preview?

Then you don't owe me anything. The preview is completely free and comes with no obligation. If you move forward, two rounds of revisions make sure every detail is right.

Can I make changes after launch?

Absolutely. Small updates are included in the Care plan. Larger changes, such as new pages or features, are quoted upfront at a fixed price before any work begins.

What's included in the Care plan?

Reliable hosting, text and photo updates, regular backups and priority fixes if anything goes wrong. It's $50 per month, billed month to month, and you can cancel anytime.
