Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what information Sonoran Wash Co. ("we", "us") collects when you visit our website, request a quote or book a service, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and service address.
  • Job details: the services you choose, property size, preferred dates and any photos you upload of the area to be cleaned.
  • Website data: basic technical information such as browser type, pages visited and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar tools.

How we use it

  • To prepare your estimate and confirm the price before any work starts.
  • To schedule, reschedule (for example on rain days) and complete your service.
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders and a request to review our work.
  • To improve our website and services.

Calls, texts and emails

By submitting a form you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message or email about your request and your service. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or HELP for help. We do not send marketing texts without your consent, and consent is not a condition of purchase.

How we share information

We never sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our scheduling, payment, email and website hosting providers, and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies, including Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, to remember your preferences and to understand how visitors use the site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will still work without them.

How long we keep it

We keep customer records for as long as needed to provide service, handle warranty questions and meet tax and accounting requirements, usually up to 3 years after your last job.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@sonoranwash.co or call (602) 555-0182 and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
