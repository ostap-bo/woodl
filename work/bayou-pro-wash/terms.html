Terms of Service

Last updated: October 1, 2026

These terms apply to estimates, bookings and services provided by Bayou Pro Wash LLC ("we", "us") in the Houston area.

Estimates

Online and photo estimates are based on the information you provide. Your final price is confirmed before work starts and you only pay the price you approve. Estimates are valid for 30 days.

Scheduling and weather

You choose an arrival window when you book. If rain, freezing temperatures or high winds make the work unsafe or less effective, we will contact you to reschedule at no cost.

Before we arrive

  • Please give us access to an outdoor water spigot.
  • Close windows and doors and move vehicles, furniture and fragile items away from the work area.
  • Let us know about loose siding, damaged surfaces or recent paint, so we can adjust our method.

Cancellations

You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment by phone, text or email.

Payment

Payment is due when the job is complete. We accept major credit and debit cards, Zelle and checks. Commercial accounts may be invoiced on agreed terms.

Our guarantee

If you are not happy with any part of the job, tell us within 7 days and we will come back and re-clean that area at no charge.
